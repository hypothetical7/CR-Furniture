What we do

Furniture and interiors, under one roof.

No handing off between a carpenter, a designer, and a contractor. One studio carries the project from concept to the last cushion.

01 / Furniture

Custom Furniture

Sofas, beds, wardrobes, dining sets, and modular storage, designed to your room's exact dimensions and made in-house.

  • Solid wood & engineered board
  • Upholstery & finishing
  • Modular kitchens & wardrobes
02 / Interiors

Interior Design

Space planning, material palettes, lighting, and 3D visualization so you see the room before a single wall is touched.

  • Space planning & layout
  • 3D concept renders
  • Material & colour selection
03 / Execution

Turnkey Execution

Civil work, electrical, false ceiling, carpentry, and finishing, managed on site so you deal with one studio, not five vendors.

  • Site supervision
  • Vendor & timeline management
  • Final styling & handover
How it works

Five steps, one studio, no surprises.

Every project — a single room or a full home — moves through the same sequence, so you always know what's next.

01

Consultation & site visit

We visit your site in Damak or nearby, take measurements, and understand how you actually use the space.

02

Design & 3D concept

Layouts, material boards, and 3D renders, refined with you until the design feels right, before anything is built.

03

Material selection & quotation

Clear, itemised quotation for materials, furniture, and labour — no vague lump sums.

04

Fabrication & execution

Furniture is built in our workshop while on-site work proceeds in parallel, tracked against a fixed timeline.

05

Handover & styling

Final walkthrough, styling touches, and a punch list closed out before we call the project done.
