How it works

Five steps, one studio, no surprises.

Every project — a single room or a full home — moves through the same sequence, so you always know what's next.

01

Consultation & site visit

We visit your site in Damak or nearby, take measurements, and understand how you actually use the space.

02

Design & 3D concept

Layouts, material boards, and 3D renders, refined with you until the design feels right, before anything is built.

03

Material selection & quotation

Clear, itemised quotation for materials, furniture, and labour — no vague lump sums.

04

Fabrication & execution

Furniture is built in our workshop while on-site work proceeds in parallel, tracked against a fixed timeline.

05

Handover & styling

Final walkthrough, styling touches, and a punch list closed out before we call the project done.

Questions

Asked before every project.

Short answers. For anything else, tap Call or WhatsApp below — you’ll reach the founder directly.

How much will my project cost?+

It depends on size and materials. Send your room size on WhatsApp for a price range, then get an exact itemised quote within 48 hours of the free site visit — no vague lump sums.

How long does it take?+

Single furniture pieces take 2–3 weeks, kitchens 3–4 weeks, and full homes 6–10 weeks. Your quotation includes a fixed timeline, tracked against workshop + site work in parallel.

Is the site visit really free?+

Yes — free in the Damak area, with measurement and a design conversation included. Nearby cities are free with a confirmed project; otherwise we adjust it against your order.

What materials do you use?+

Solid wood and branded engineered boards with laminate, PU, or veneer finishes — plus quartz options for kitchens. Every material is named in your quote, so you know exactly what you’re paying for.

Do I get any after-service?+

Yes. Manufacturing defects are fixed, hinges and channels are adjusted after settling, and you keep one direct contact — the founder — instead of chasing vendors.

Which areas do you serve?+

Our studio is in Damak. We regularly work in Birtamod, Itahari, Dharan, and Biratnagar — and take projects beyond on request.
